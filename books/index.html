An ongoing series · Set in Harmon

The books.

Twenty novels, one small town. Each is a self-contained warning, and each one stands completely alone, the cost of stopping is something every protagonist could not pay either. The order below is a suggestion for a first journey through Harmon, not a requirement. Begin anywhere. You will meet these people again, in someone else’s book.

A good place to start · reads standalone

The Good Father

David Marsh · history teacher, Harmon High

David Marsh is a good father, a respected history teacher, and a son determined to keep his mother in the care home she knows. When the bills outgrow his salary, a winning sports-betting model seems to offer one clean way through.

The odds were calculated. The cost was not.

The Honest Woman

Ruth Calloway · town clerk

Town clerk Ruth Calloway trusts records because paper stays where people put it. In Cabinet Twenty-Two, two sets of water reports refuse to agree: Calloway-Harmon's filings show safe cadmium levels. State tests downstream do not.

The numbers were wrong for twenty years. She is the first to say so.

The Quiet Man

Boyd Haskell · hardware-store owner

Boyd Haskell has kept his family hardware store open for thirty-one years. Its brass counter, old bell, and empty oak stool hold memories of his father—and of Tyler, the son he buried eleven years ago. When a chain store comes to Harmon, the numbers say Haskell Hardware cannot survive.

He kept the store open. Grief kept everything else still.

The Loyal Man

Dennis Reilly · county property assessor

County assessor Dennis Reilly has spent sixteen years making sure every number can survive the next question. Then his older brother Kevin asks for one small favor: move a late property appeal back four months and save him twenty-three thousand dollars.

He has never told his brother no. This time, yes leaves a paper trail.

The Tired Mother

Cat Brennan · ER physician, St. Clare’s

Emergency physician Catherine Brennan knows how to decide who needs her first. At St. Clare's, that instinct saves lives. At home, it keeps her racing between night shifts and the dementia care she promised her dying ex-husband—while sixteen-year-old Miles grows quieter behind a half-closed door.

She can care for everyone. She cannot see who needs her most.

The Lawyer Daughter

Diana Torres · corporate attorney

Corporate lawyer Diana Torres has built a career finding what other people hide. After her father's funeral, a brass key opens a locked drawer in his office—and four hundred eighty checks to the same woman.

Her father left forty years of payments. The names are still alive.

The Tending Woman

Elena Voss · retired librarian, widow

Elena Voss has spent her life tending what other people need: a garden, a library, a grieving friend, and Adam, the grown son whose next venture is always one step from working.

She has spent a lifetime giving. This time, it may cost her everything.

Maggie Caldwell · contractor, Caldwell Construction

Thirty employees depend on Maggie Caldwell and the construction company her father built. When two failed projects leave Caldwell weeks from collapse, a forty-unit development offers the one contract that can save it.

Saving the company takes one signature. Living with it takes everything else.

The Saved Woman

Nora Finch · manager, Harmon Harbor Marina

Seventeen years ago, Victor Salk pulled Nora Finch from Lake Harmon and gave her every day that followed. When he asks for a character statement in a custody fight, Nora believes one careful paragraph is the least she owes him.

He saved her life. Now he wants her truth.

The Accused Man

Jack Callahan · the returning exile

Jack Callahan left Harmon twenty-five years ago after Amy Weston vanished. Police corroborated his alibi and never charged him. The town reached its own verdict.

The town never proved he did it. It never stopped believing.

The Steady Hand

Neil Mercer · neurosurgeon, St. Clare’s

Dr. Mercer has spent twenty-one years at St. Clare's making decisions measured in millimeters. When his right hand begins to tremble, the episodes are brief, private, and easy to explain away. No patient has been harmed.

His hands have never failed him. Until now.

The Ambitious Son

Marcus Cole · county road maintenance

Councilman Marcus Cole built his career by making government notice the people it overlooks. A statehouse seat could let him do more—if he can unseat the powerful incumbent whose family once employed his mother.

One leak can win the election. The whole truth can end it.

The Design Partner

Anne Loring · architect, Loring & Finch

Architect Anne Loring has spent twenty years building a respected practice with Paul Finch. She designs what carries the load. He manages the contracts and money. Thirty-five employees trust the structure they created.

The invoices add up. The partnership does not.

The Distant Man

Frank Morrow · the returning son

Machinist Frank Morrow trusts what can be measured. When his mother's stroke calls him back to Harmon, he returns after fifteen years away with one plan: apologize to the family he left behind.

He came home to be a father. His family learned to live without one.

The Silent Pastor

David Rourke · pastor, First Baptist

Pastor David Rourke has served First Baptist for twenty-four years. For the last three, he has felt no God behind his prayers. Still, the sermons remain correct, the sick are visited, and nobody notices the fourth point has vanished from every page.

He still knows what to say. He no longer knows what he believes.

The Distant Husband

James Ota · graphic designer

Designer James Ota knows how to give other people's ideas a visible form. Six days before marrying architect Nina, a stranger describes the marriage they have not yet begun: the deadlines, the locked bathroom, the therapy door he will almost reach, and the daughter who will learn to make her face calm.

He promised this marriage would be different. He brought the same walls.

The Watchful Teacher

Warren Gibbs · English teacher, Harmon High

For twenty-six years, English teacher Warren Gibbs has taken attendance twice: once for the school, once for the frightened, hungry, or hurting child behind the name. When junior Jess Harlow arrives with bruises, long sleeves, and an essay about a door that will not lock, Warren reports what he sees. Three investigations return the same answer: unfounded.

The system said nothing happened. He decided to act anyway.

The Merciful Nurse

Luisa Dominguez · ICU nurse, St. Clare’s

ICU nurse Luisa Dominguez knows suffering does not fit neatly inside a pain scale. When dying patient Harold Gaines keeps whispering please while his sons delay comfort care, Luisa sees what the chart cannot: his final hours are being spent inside a decision no one can resolve fast enough.

She knows how to stop pain. She must decide where mercy ends.

The Reluctant Witness

Rachel Kim · software engineer

Software engineer Rachel Kim sees a man shot behind a riverside restaurant. Under the third orange streetlamp, the killer turns his face toward her for less than one second. The scar on his cheek is enough to identify Anthony Caruso—and perhaps enough to convict him.

Her testimony saves the case. It may cost her marriage.

The Good Writer

Claire Novak · novelist

Novelist Claire Novak has spent three years turning her sister Meg's addiction and recovery into her best book. She changed names, dates, and places—everything except the pain that makes the pages true. Meg knows none of it.

The story could make her career. It belongs to her sister.
