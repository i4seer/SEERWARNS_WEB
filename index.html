The series

Quiet stories. Exact consequences.

Seer Warns is psychological rather than supernatural spectacle: households, jobs, marriages, loyalties, money, secrets, and the moment a person chooses the loss they can live with over the one they cannot.

1

The Seer sits down.

A diner booth, a bus, a dock, a hallway. The world goes a half-beat quiet and a stranger speaks.

2

One impossible detail.

The warning contains the private fact that makes disbelief impossible. No prophecy theatrics. Just certainty.

3

The choice comes anyway.

Stopping would cost something the protagonist believes they cannot surrender. So they continue, warned.

The shared setting

One town holds every story.

The Lamplighter booth, Haskell Hardware, St. Clare’s, the Calloway–Harmon plant, Bishop Hill, the waterfront, the roads out. A place named once is the same place every time.

Read a few books and the background stops being background. A nurse in one story becomes the center of another. A building that survives one choice carries the cost of the next.
